Testimonials

I have known Sheree Bergman for many years, and I can say that her book comes at the right time when so many people are hurting, feeling hopeless, and without answers. Her book provides answers and instructions for inner healing like few books do. I have personally benefited greatly from her ministry in times past, and I can confidently say that the content of her book has the knowledge, the instructions, the love, and the power to heal the broken-hearted and set the captives free.

Sheree has wisely and lovingly organized her book and workbook in such a way that it is easy to follow and, most of all, uses her life story, not only as an example of the pain and struggle one can suffer, but also of the hope, healing, and victory that can be experienced.

Sheree has many certifications, but what stands out as the great qualifying factor for this timely book is how God stepped in, healing in her own life one step at a time.

I recommend her amazing and powerful book to everyone, especially believers, pastors, and all who need inner healing. I've chosen to incorporate her book and workbook as part of the curriculum in my school.

Lawrence DeSantis, OM, ND, WD
Ordained Minister / Author / Naturopath /
New Eden School Founder
